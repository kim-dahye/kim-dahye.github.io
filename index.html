Dahye Kim

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science · Boston University

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Boston University, advised by Deepti Ghadiyaram.

My research focuses on image and video generation and multimodal large language models, with an emphasis on efficiency and interpretability. I study how these models work and use that understanding to make them more efficient and controllable.

I am currently a research intern at Adobe and previously interned at Amazon. Before joining Boston University, I received my master’s degree from Yonsei University under the supervision of Kwanghoon Sohn.
